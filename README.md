# Apple Global Sales Insight Dashboard 🍏📊

## Overview
This project showcases an end-to-end data analysis and visualization process for Apple's global sales data. By cleaning and transforming raw data, the project provides an interactive dashboard designed to monitor sales performance and generate actionable insights for data-driven business decisions.

## Tech Stack
* **Tools:** Power BI, Microsoft Excel (Power Query)
* **Languages:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)

## Key Features & Contributions
* **Data Preparation:** Cleaned, transformed, and modeled 11,501 sales records using Power Query to ensure data accuracy and consistency.
* **DAX Implementation:** Developed custom DAX measures to calculate and track key business performance indicators (KPIs).
* **Data Visualization:** Built a dynamic and interactive Power BI dashboard featuring KPI cards, trend analysis charts, geographical maps, and slicers for in-depth sales monitoring.
* **Business Insights:** Extracted meaningful and actionable insights from the dashboard to support strategic decision-making.

### 3. Profitability Analysis
* **Financial Summary:** The dashboard displays a total revenue of $288.90T alongside estimated profits totaling 5.41M.
* **Profit Margin by Product:** The green bar chart ranks products by profitability (Margin) from highest to lowest.
* **💡 Business Insight:** Although Pro series products generate the highest revenue, products like the Apple Watch SE (2nd Gen) and iPad Mini (6th Gen) yield the most outstanding profit margins. Meanwhile, the MacBook Pro and iPhone lines maintain high and consistent margins.

### 5. In-Depth Analysis: Revenue Trends & Customer Segments
* **Revenue Movement (Combo Chart):** The green bar chart shows revenue over time (2022-2024), highlighting spikes that may align with new product launches. The %YoY line (blue) helps track growth performance closely.
* **Customer Segments:** Revenue is divided into four main categories: Education, Government, Business, and Individual.
* **💡 Business Insight:** All four customer segments contribute nearly equal proportions of revenue. This reflects excellent "Customer Diversification," ensuring business stability without over-reliance on a single group.
